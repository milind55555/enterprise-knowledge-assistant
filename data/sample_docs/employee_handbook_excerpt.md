# Employee Handbook Excerpt

Welcome to Northwind Systems. This excerpt summarises the policies you will use most often. Read the full policy documents for details.

## Annual leave
Every full-time employee gets 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave accrues monthly, at 2 days per month. Employees can carry over a maximum of 5 unused days into the next calendar year. Any balance above 5 days lapses on 31 December.

## Working hours
Core working hours are 10:00 to 16:00 IST. Employees are expected to be reachable during core hours. Standard working hours are 8 hours per day, Monday to Friday.
