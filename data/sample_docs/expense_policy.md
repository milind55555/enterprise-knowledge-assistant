# Travel and Expense Policy

## Submitting expenses
Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense date. Receipts are required for any single expense above 500 INR.

## Approvals
Managers can approve claims up to 25,000 INR. Claims above 25,000 INR need director approval.

## Travel limits
The domestic meal allowance is 1,500 INR per day. Hotel stays are capped at 7,000 INR per night in metro cities. Flights shorter than 6 hours must be booked in economy class.
