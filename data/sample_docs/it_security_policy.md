# IT Security Policy

## Passwords and authentication
Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for all company accounts, including email, VPN and the cloud console. Passwords must never be shared or written down.

## Devices
All laptops use full-disk encryption. USB storage devices are blocked by default. A lost or stolen device must be reported to the IT helpdesk within 2 hours.

## Network
Employees must use the company VPN when connected to public Wi-Fi.

## Phishing and incidents
Suspected phishing emails must be reported to security@northwind.example within 1 hour of receipt. Do not click links or open attachments in suspicious emails.

## Access reviews
System access rights are reviewed every quarter. Managers must confirm or remove access for their team members during each review.
