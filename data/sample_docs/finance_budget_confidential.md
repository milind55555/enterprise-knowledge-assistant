# FY2026 Budget (Confidential)

## Department budgets
The FY2026 research and development budget is 12 crore INR. The marketing budget is 4.5 crore INR. The cloud infrastructure spend cap is 2.8 crore INR.

## Purchasing
Any purchase above 10 lakh INR needs CFO approval. Standard vendor payment terms are net 45 days.

## Reviews
The budget is reviewed quarterly, in the first week after each quarter ends.
