# New Joiner Onboarding Guide

## First day
Your laptop is delivered before your start date so that it is ready on day 1. IT will set up your accounts and MFA during the first morning.

## Buddy programme
Every new joiner is paired with a buddy for the first 4 weeks. The buddy answers day-to-day questions and introduces you to the team.

## Mandatory training
Three trainings must be completed within 14 days of joining: security awareness, code of conduct and POSH (prevention of sexual harassment).

## 30-60-90 day plan
Your manager agrees a 30-60-90 day plan with you in the first week. The plan is reviewed at the end of each period.

## Probation
The probation period is 6 months. A confirmation review happens in the final month of probation.
