# Engineering On-Call Handbook

## Rotation
On-call rotates weekly. The handover happens every Monday at 10:00 IST. The outgoing engineer shares open incidents and risky changes with the incoming engineer.

## Severity levels and response times
A Sev1 incident must be acknowledged within 5 minutes. A Sev2 incident must be acknowledged within 15 minutes. A Sev3 incident is handled by the next business day.

## Escalation
If the on-call engineer does not acknowledge a page within 15 minutes, it escalates automatically to the engineering manager.

## Communication
During a Sev1 incident, the status page must be updated every 30 minutes until the incident is resolved.

## Postmortems
A written postmortem is required within 5 business days for every Sev1 and Sev2 incident. Postmortems are blameless and focus on process and system fixes.

## On-call allowance
Engineers receive an on-call allowance of 8,000 INR for each week they are on call.
