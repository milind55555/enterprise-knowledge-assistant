# Remote Work Policy

## Hybrid model
Northwind follows a hybrid model. Employees work from the office on Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday. Monday and Friday are work-from-home days by default.

## Fully remote roles
A fully remote arrangement needs written approval from both the employee's manager and HR. Approval is reviewed every 6 months.

## Home office support
New employees receive a one-time home office stipend of 30,000 INR. Northwind also reimburses internet costs of up to 1,000 INR per month against a bill.

## Working from another country
Employees may work from another country for up to 20 days per calendar year. The request must be approved by the manager and HR at least 30 days before travel, because of tax and legal checks.
