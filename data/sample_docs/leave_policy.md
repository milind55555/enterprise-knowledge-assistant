# Leave Policy

## Annual leave
Every full-time employee gets 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave accrues monthly, at 2 days per month. Employees can carry over a maximum of 5 unused days into the next calendar year. Any balance above 5 days lapses on 31 December.

## Sick leave
Employees get 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required if sick leave runs for more than 2 consecutive days.

## Parental leave
The primary caregiver gets 16 weeks of paid parental leave. The secondary caregiver gets 6 weeks of paid parental leave. Parental leave must be taken within 12 months of the birth or adoption.

## How to apply
Leave requests are submitted through the HR portal at least 5 working days in advance. Your manager approves or rejects the request within 2 working days. Unpaid leave of any length needs approval from a director.
