# Compensation and Performance (Confidential)

## Salary bands
Level L1 has a salary band of 6 to 9 LPA. Level L2 has a band of 9 to 14 LPA. Level L3 has a band of 14 to 22 LPA. Level L4 has a band of 22 to 35 LPA.

## Annual bonus
The target annual bonus is 10 percent of base pay for levels L1 and L2, 15 percent for L3, and 20 percent for L4.

## Appraisal cycle
The annual appraisal cycle runs in April. Salary revisions take effect from 1 May.

## Performance improvement plan
A performance improvement plan (PIP) lasts 60 days. HR must be involved from the first meeting.
